Privacy Policy

Outbound: Arrow Puzzle · Developer: KINDIX DEVS · Last updated: October 28, 2026

In short: Outbound does not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email, phone number or location. Your game progress stays on your device. The app shows ads (Google AdMob) and, when purchases are available, uses Google Play and RevenueCat to process them. These partners may collect limited device information as described below.

1. Who we are

Outbound: Arrow Puzzle ("Outbound", "the app", "we", "us") is a mobile puzzle game published by KINDIX DEVS. If you have any question about this policy, contact us at kindixdevs@gmail.com.

2. Information we do not collect

We do not ask for, and do not collect: your name, email address, phone number, home address, contacts, photos, microphone or camera data, or precise location. There are no user accounts in Outbound.

3. Information stored on your device

To let you continue where you left off, the app saves your game data on your device: your level, coins, badges, settings (theme, sound, vibration), chosen look, best scores, reminder times and simple play statistics (for example arrows cleared and play time). This data is not sent to us. It is removed if you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

Outbound shows banner, interstitial and rewarded video ads provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may collect and use information such as your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app information, and ad interactions. Depending on your region and choices, ads may be personalised or non-personalised.

  • In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and similar regions, we show a consent form (Google's User Messaging Platform) where you can choose how your data is used for ads.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalised ads, in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads).
  • Learn more: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and AdMob data collection.

Players can remove ads by buying Premium or the Remove ads purchase, when available.

5. In-app purchases

When you buy Premium, Remove ads or coin packs, the payment is handled by Google Play. We never see or store your payment card details. We use RevenueCat to confirm and manage purchases. RevenueCat may process an anonymous app user ID, your purchase history for this app and basic device information so that your purchases are recognised and restored. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

6. Notifications

If you turn on daily reminders, the app schedules up to three local notifications on your device at the times you choose. These are created on your phone and do not require us to collect any personal data. You can turn them off at any time in Settings > Daily reminders, or in your phone's notification settings.

8. Children's privacy

Outbound is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us at kindixdevs@gmail.com and we will help resolve it. Parents can also limit ad personalisation in their child's Android settings.

9. Data retention and deletion

Game data stays on your device until you clear it or uninstall the app. You can erase your progress at any time in Settings > Reset progress. Because we do not hold your personal data on our own servers, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. To ask about data held by our partners (for example purchase records), contact us and we will help, or contact the partner directly using the links above.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EEA, the UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal data, to object to certain processing, and to withdraw consent. You can change your ad consent choices in the app's consent form when it is shown, and in your device settings. To exercise any right, email kindixdevs@gmail.com.

11. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information, and we keep as little as possible. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

12. Third-party links

The app may open links to other services, such as the Google Play Store to rate the app or your email app to contact support. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of other services.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will also be shown in the app or on the Google Play listing. Using the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
